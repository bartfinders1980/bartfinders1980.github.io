THE BAND

Resonate is a rock cover band from the south of the Netherlands; four musicians who joined forces, sharing the same goal, which is to play rock music as it should be played: voice, drums, bass and guitar – firm, thick and with genuine passion!

Many years of musical experience, each member having played in a myriad of other bands, allows them to play a wide range of songs, appealing to a broad audience and still allowing for some unexpected ‘twists’. Frequently heard feedback: "a summary of the Pinkpop festival over the years" – everything from Placebo to Pearl Jam or the Foo Fighters to the Editors can be expected during a gig.
